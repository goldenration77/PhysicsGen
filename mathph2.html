Unit 1 · 12 Hours

Fourier Series

1.1 Periodic Functions & Orthogonality

A function \( f(x) \) is periodic with period \( T \) if \( f(x+T) = f(x) \) for all \( x \). The fundamental period is the smallest positive such \( T \).

Orthogonality of Sine and Cosine

On \([-\pi, \pi]\):

\[ \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin(mx)\sin(nx)\,dx = \pi \delta_{mn} \quad (m,n \geq 1) \] \[ \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \cos(mx)\cos(nx)\,dx = \pi \delta_{mn} \quad (m,n \geq 1),\quad 2\pi \text{ if } m=n=0 \] \[ \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin(mx)\cos(nx)\,dx = 0 \]

1.2 Dirichlet Conditions

If \( f(x) \) is \( 2\pi \)-periodic and in one period is absolutely integrable, has finitely many extrema and jump discontinuities, then the Fourier series converges to \( f(x) \) at continuity points and to the average of left/right limits at jumps.

1.3 Fourier Coefficients

\[ f(x) \sim \frac{a_0}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \bigl( a_n \cos(nx) + b_n \sin(nx) \bigr) \] \[ a_n = \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\cos(nx)\,dx, \quad b_n = \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\sin(nx)\,dx \]

1.4 Complex Form

\[ f(x) \sim \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} c_n e^{inx}, \qquad c_n = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)e^{-inx}\,dx \]

1.5 Even & Odd Functions

Even: \( f(-x)=f(x) \)

Only cosine terms. \( b_n = 0 \).

Odd: \( f(-x)=-f(x) \)

Only sine terms. \( a_n = 0 \).

1.6 Parseval’s Identity

\[ \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} [f(x)]^2\,dx = \frac{a_0^2}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(a_n^2 + b_n^2) \]

Unit 2 · 14 Hours

Frobenius Method & Special Functions

2.1 Singular Points

For \( y'' + P(x)y' + Q(x)y = 0 \), \( x_0 \) is ordinary if \( P,Q \) analytic there; otherwise singular. Regular singularity if \( (x-x_0)P \) and \( (x-x_0)^2 Q \) analytic.

2.2 Method of Frobenius

\[ y = x^r \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n x^n, \quad a_0 \neq 0 \]

Indicial equation for \( r \), then recurrence for \( a_n \).

2.3 Legendre Equation

\[ (1-x^2)y'' - 2xy' + \ell(\ell+1)y = 0 \]

Key Properties

  • Rodrigues: \( P_\ell(x) = \frac{1}{2^\ell \ell!} \frac{d^\ell}{dx^\ell}(x^2-1)^\ell \)
  • Generating fn: \( (1-2xt+t^2)^{-1/2} = \sum P_\ell(x) t^\ell \)
  • Orthogonality: \( \int_{-1}^{1} P_\ell P_m \,dx = \frac{2}{2\ell+1}\delta_{\ell m} \)

2.4 Bessel Functions

\[ x^2 y'' + xy' + (x^2 - \nu^2)y = 0, \quad J_n(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^k}{k!(k+n)!}\Bigl(\frac{x}{2}\Bigr)^{2k+n} \]

Zeros of \( J_n \) crucial for cylindrical boundary-value problems.

Unit 3 · 2 Hours

Dirac Delta Function

3.1 Definition

\[ \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x)\delta(x-a)\,dx = f(a) \]

3.2 Representations

Gaussian

\[ \delta(x) = \lim_{\sigma\to 0^+} \frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-x^2/(2\sigma^2)} \]

Rectangular

\[ \delta(x) = \lim_{\varepsilon\to 0^+} \frac{1}{\varepsilon}\Pi\Bigl(\frac{x}{\varepsilon}\Bigr) \]

3.3 Properties

  • \( \delta(-x) = \delta(x) \)
  • \( x\delta(x) = 0 \)
  • \( \delta(ax) = \frac{1}{|a|}\delta(x) \)
  • \( \int f(x)\delta'(x)\,dx = -f'(0) \)

Unit 4 · 4 Hours

Some Special Integrals

4.1 Gamma Function

\[ \Gamma(z) = \int_0^{\infty} t^{z-1}e^{-t}\,dt, \quad \Re(z)>0 \] \[ \Gamma(z+1) = z\Gamma(z), \qquad \Gamma(n+1) = n! \]

4.2 Beta Function

\[ B(m,n) = \int_0^1 t^{m-1}(1-t)^{n-1}\,dt = \frac{\Gamma(m)\Gamma(n)}{\Gamma(m+n)} \]

4.3 Error Function

\[ \mathrm{erf}(x) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_0^x e^{-t^2}\,dt \]

Unit 5 · 2 Hours

Theory of Errors

5.1 Systematic vs Random

Systematic

Reproducible biases. Often correctable.

Random

Fluctuations. Reduced by averaging.

5.2 Propagation

\[ \sigma_q^2 \approx \Bigl(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\Bigr)^2\sigma_x^2 + \Bigl(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\Bigr)^2\sigma_y^2 + \cdots \]

5.3 Normal Law

\[ P(x)\,dx = \frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-(x-\mu)^2/(2\sigma^2)}\,dx \]

Standard error of mean: \( \sigma/\sqrt{N} \). Probable error ≈ \( 0.6745\,\sigma \).

Unit 6

Partial Differential Equations

6.1 Separation of Variables

Assume product solution; PDE → ODEs linked by separation constant. Boundary conditions quantize eigenvalues.

6.2 Laplace’s Equation

\[ \nabla^2 u = 0 \]

Rectangular: sine/cosine eigenfunctions.

Cylindrical: Bessel in radial direction.

Spherical: Legendre / spherical harmonics; radial \( r^\ell \), \( r^{-(\ell+1)} \).

6.3 Wave Equation

\[ \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} = c^2 \nabla^2 u \]

String, rectangular membrane (double Fourier), circular membrane (Bessel zeros).
