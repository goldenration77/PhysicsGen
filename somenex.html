Artificial Transmutation & Artificial Radioactivity
50+ Nuclear Disintegration / Reaction Examples

Key: • All reactions conserve mass number (A) and atomic number (Z).
• Projectiles: α = 4He, n = neutron, p = proton, d = deuteron (2H), γ = gamma.
• Many products are radioactive (artificial radioactivity).

Practice Tip: For any reaction, always check:
1. Sum of mass numbers (A) on both sides equal
2. Sum of atomic numbers (Z) on both sides equal
3. Identify if the product nucleus is radioactive and its decay mode (β⁻, β⁺, EC, etc.)
